How we got here

five years of building, tested by three market cycles.

2021

Pilot launch

Opened with a €10,000 minimum and 40 founding investors, deployed into a narrow basket of blue-chip liquidity pairs.

2022

First full market cycle

Navigated a sharp drawdown; active rebalancing kept losses well below passive liquidity-provider benchmarks.

2023

Independent audit and licensing

Completed a third-party smart contract audit and registered as a regulated fund manager in two jurisdictions.

2024

Institutional allocations open

Introduced a dedicated tier for family offices and institutional investors alongside the original individual pool.

2025

€400M AUM milestone

Crossed €400M under management with a waitlist for new capital for the first time.

2026

Today

1,140 investors, five years of published performance history, and a research team of twelve.
